---
question: What happens when I skip a Pomodoro?
order: 2
---

It depends on how much of the Pomodoro has passed. If you skip after 75% of its planned length, it counts as completed and the session indicator moves forward. If you skip before that point, the indicator does not move forward. The planned length is the original duration, before you shorten or extend the countdown.

Either way, the time you spent is recorded in your statistics.
